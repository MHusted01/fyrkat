# Deviations from the approved tooling, for owner review

**Status: recorded for review. Nothing here is approved.** The owner's message of 2026-11-08 says to
document these as deviations and not to treat that message as retroactive blanket approval. Each row
says what was done, why, what it affects, and how to undo it. "Owner-approved" means the owner answered
that specific question in the working session on 2026-11-08.
