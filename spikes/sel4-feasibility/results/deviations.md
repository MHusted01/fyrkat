# Deviations from the approved tooling, for owner review

**This list is not an approval.** The owner's message of 2026-11-08 says to document these as
deviations requiring review and not to treat that message as retroactive blanket approval. A row marked
**owner-approved** records one specific answer the owner gave in the working session on 2026-11-08 (see
the plan's Amendments). Every other row, and every detail of a row that is not named as approved, is
**not approved**. Each row says what was done, why, and how to undo it.

**Update, later on 2026-11-08.** The owner then approved D2, D3, D7, D9, D10 and D14(b), each **for the isolated S1
research scope only** and not as a precedent (see the plan's last Amendments entry). Those rows say so in their Status
column, after the earlier text, which is kept as history with two exceptions: D3's earlier text overstated a prior
approval and is marked as corrected in its row, and D7's "Not named" became "Not named at the time". No other row
changed. D17 and D18, added after E1 ran, were approved the same way. D25 (release-variant tooling), D26 (Route I tooling) and D27 (A5 step 0 tooling), added on 2026-11-10 under the owner's message of 2026-11-09, are described in their rows; they were reviewed independently on 2026-11-10 and their findings fixed. D28 (release-kernel execution), D29 (A5 step 1) and D30 (A5 step 2), added on 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11 under the owner's message of 2026-11-10, are described in their rows. D24, added with A2a on 2026-11-09 (authorized as routine tooling, not independently reviewed), is described in its row.
